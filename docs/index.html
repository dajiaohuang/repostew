A portable Skill for multi-agent harnesses: assemble Workers within scope and authority for discovery, investigation, delivery and ongoing follow-up. Keep state in Markdown.

Three membership lists

New targets, complete historical membership and followed groups. Lists hold identities only; groups may overlap, and scans deduplicate their union.

Minimal shared facts

Each project writer maintains root state.md and local work/work.md. Coordinator owns globals; one publisher serializes Git. unfinished.md is navigation only, not complete scope or authority.

One project, one directory

Use state/repositories/<host>/<namespace>/<repo>/. Share reviewed state.md; ignore local work/ with notes, multiple checkouts and tmp. Never recursively upload or scan execution material as state.

Independent, private sources

Public source positions live in read-positions.md; mail positions stay private. Preserve native boundaries and read source bodies live, without copying an interaction database.

Complete scope.
Concise assignments.

Establish outcome, scope, authority and resources. The Coordinator uses assembly rules and templates to assign Workers. Dispatch, wait, integrate, continue, hand off or recover through native harness capabilities; continue by delta.

Load rules by task and action: reuse supplied current text, batch missing startup reads, load conditional methods before their actions. Links are navigation, not recursive reads. Leaves need their boundaries, not full Coordinator briefs. Capture validates sources; metadata-only work needs no clone/tests; repairs keep affected and mandatory checks. Coordinator checks scope/coverage, not a second engineering acceptance. Permission, privacy, mutation reconciliation and preservation gates remain.

Five forms: discovery/admission, new audits, contribution reassessment, incremental feedback, recurring patrols. Capture and deduplicate followed issues, notifications/selected mail and open/unfinished objects under separate source positions.

Trace actual use and documents; establish contribution evidence. Discuss ordinary unsettled contracts. Every new security finding uses private reporting only, never public issues; retain protected evidence and blockers without an authorized private channel. Complete assigned audit coverage before grouped repair, validation and publication under target and safety gates; handle feedback, consolidate roots or withdraw within authority.

Maintainers perform authorized maintenance: triage issues, review/amend/merge PRs under current rights/settings, audit actual use/documents and revise code. Keep actual-use coverage and essential CI/tests. Assigned PR review adds no universal pre-publication gate.

Write minimal records; migrate only when authorized; reconcile, recover or reclassify history; publish reviewed backups when requested. Check resource disposition for resources used or assigned, including blocked/no-change passes; omit empty resource ceremonies. Separate coverage, delivery, resolution and release.

Keep authority explicit, execution serial by default and one writer per resource. Apply user-selected exclusions before selecting or continuing GitHub work; preserve existing objects/history. Prohibit defensive programming, trust internal contracts and retain necessary boundary checks. Trust usable finals, preserve private/unknown work and report incomplete outcomes accurately.

Harnesses supply execution; forge rules map native access. Roles own responsibilities, prompts own wording, and workspace Markdown owns state. Files grant no implicit authority, tasks or schedules.

Bounded patrols.
Reliable continuation.

Configurable baseline: hourly notifications/selected mail, followed issues every 6 hours, open/unfinished-object fallback every 24 hours. Continue unfinished batches before new ones; reuse unchanged evidence.

Advance positions only after complete capture and handling or reliable retention. Never send mail, change read/subscription state or expand mailbox scope. Cadence does not prove coverage. Paused jobs remain paused; private material, backups and resource disposition retain their authority boundaries.
