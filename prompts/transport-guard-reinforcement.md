# Offline transport-guard reinforcement

Specialize [temporary reinforcement](reinforcement.md) when a capture helper has a demonstrated
stream-counting, size-limit, timeout or reader-lifecycle defect. Use the shared C/W header plus one
existing engineering form; this supplement neither resumes a stopped repository nor changes its limits.

> Input: {exact failed helper/fixture paths and native failure; preserve originals/raw}.
> Owner/write boundary: {responsible C; one Worker; explicitly assigned task-local shared-resource path}.
> Outcome: {reusable capture implementation, deterministic fixtures, byte contract and residual limits}.
> Limits: {zero network/Git/install; source/raw caps; fixture/process and continuous elapsed ceilings}.
> Adoption: {future independently authorized units only; affected original units remain stopped unless
> separately authorized}. Do not overwrite shared active tools or retrofit old compliance claims.

- Specify whether the budget covers observed bytes, saved raw and combined stdout/stderr; enforce
  the required total prospectively across readers, not by post-capture assertion or truncation.
- Exercise normal, concurrent split streams, oversized chunks, exact-cap/empty output, timeouts,
  nonzero exits and reader failures with bounded local emitter processes. Preserve partial raw and
  join/stop every owned reader/process. Reaching the cap may conservatively mean INCOMPLETE,
  never fabricated success or complete response evidence.
- Reproduce the known defect as an expected negative fixture; validate the repaired contract without
  live requests, target engineering, credentials, host scans or dependency/toolchain changes.
- Distinguish fixture pass, transport completeness and target acceptance. Preserve original failures,
  unknown response/accounting and deadlines; a new helper is not permission to replay the old request.
- Hand back once through the C with fixture outcomes and safe adoption boundaries; no automatic
  retry, second correction, deployment into active tasks or new monitoring service.
