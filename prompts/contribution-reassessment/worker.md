# Contribution reassessment — W input/coverage

Use the [W entry](../worker-start.md) and [one task](../task.md); apply the [reassessment form](../../references/work-forms/contribution-reassessment.md).
- Input: exact authored objects or whole-repository selection, base/head, prior proof and unfinished links.
- Coverage: changed/disputed/missing evidence and each object's retain/repair/consolidate/authorized-withdraw/block
  disposition. Preserve merged/others' work. Deliver necessary corrections, not merely a review summary.
- No fresh full audit/tests for unchanged metadata; pending/cancelled CI alone is not failure.
