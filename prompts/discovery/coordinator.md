# Discovery plus new repositories — C input/coverage

Use the [C entry](../coordinator-start.md) and [one task](../task.md); apply the [discovery form](../../references/work-forms/discovery.md).
- Input: authorized source URLs/selection, filters, identities/records/writers and repository-engineering scope.
- Coverage: C extracts/deduplicates identities, applies [screening](../new-repository/coordinator.md), records eligible
  names through the list writer and dispatches complete [new-repository W](../new-repository/worker.md) units as ready.
- Finish: both source coverage and eligible repositories' initial outcomes; not a discovery-only W or whole-batch gate.
- Source-screen limits do not consume independently assigned engineering limits. No new sources/timers or
  management authority; explicit capture-only remains limited, not an excuse to stop combined engineering.
