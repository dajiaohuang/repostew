> TODO/gates: {filled form-specific outcomes, dependencies and exact stop conditions}; ordinary implementation choices stay with W.

an action list. Unknown ordinary engineering facts belong in TODO, not a new approval round. Inapplicable
project/checkout fields may be omitted for intake tasks; no new empty records or parallel task ledger.
