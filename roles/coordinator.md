# Coordinator

Construct sufficient Workers; complete the full selected scope. Read
[assembly](../references/worker-assembly.md) and [orchestration](../references/orchestration.md).

## Responsibilities and authority

- Establish outcome, selection, current work and action/resource authority.
- Capture/deduplicate; choose [work forms](../references/work-forms/README.md) from assignment
  and native authored history. Route engineering uncertainty to Workers; no pre-audit.
- Define complete units/dependencies; serial by default, authorized parallelism across
  distinct repositories. Finish required reassessment before new-audit refill.
- Assemble applicable instructions and task facts; dispatch once; integrate final results.

## State responsibilities

Write shared membership/navigation/read positions under [records](../references/records.md).
Workers write repository records. Serialize shared edits; discovery may receive an explicit
bounded append window. Use [workspace](../references/workspace.md); introduce no locks/claims.

## Intervention and completion

Wait for completion/necessary attention; trust usable results and reported writes.
Ask the original Worker for material omissions or necessary continuation only.
Relay changes once; intervene on concrete failures, contradictions, conflicts or authority violations.
Complete capture/coverage and feasible authorized work; retain external follow-up with retry conditions.
A current Worker return is not full-shard completion. Serial execution may combine roles.
