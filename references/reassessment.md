# Single-repository contribution reassessment

Use [delivery](workflows/delivery.md) and [necessity standards](contribution-value.md).
This is assigned engineering review, not Coordinator acceptance or another default full audit.

## Capture the assigned objects

Establish native identity, author, scope and authority. Whole-repository review fully paginates authored
issues/PRs across all states and reconciles all authoritative unfinished links, including nonmembers.
Use closed/merged history for duplicates, fixes, decisions/regressions; reuse unchanged handling.
Targeted feedback keeps its object set. Comments are not authorship. Split capped searches;
unavailable/truncated is not empty coverage. Start with live metadata/base/head/diff/current-head CI;
deepen changed, unknown or feasible unresolved work only. No repeat clone/audit/tests by default.
Pending/cancelled/action-required checks are not passes or withdrawal grounds alone.

## Decide and complete the engineering action

Trace supported use/contract and affected results; separate real repair from defenses/helper artifacts/cosmetics.
All new security findings use [private reporting only](security.md). For ordinary work, after bounded evidence review:

- Keep/repair: error remains; handle feedback, remove unsupported parts, correct claims, minimally fix/validate,
  update the existing PR and ready eligible Drafts. A wrong patch does not invalidate a real error.
- Consolidate: retain necessary fixes/context in matching objects before authorized duplicate withdrawal;
  independent errors remain distinct unless one coherent repair requires them together.
- Withdraw within authority: unnecessary/incorrect, fixed, superseded, scoped-rejected or necessity unestablished.
  Use "necessity not established" where appropriate; never invent "no defect"/"fixed".
- Discuss/retain: evidenced unsettled contract or unavailable required evidence/access; keep exact question
  or blocker/retry, continue independent work. Unsupported suspicion is not a discussion justification;
  an unsettled question alone does not justify a repair PR.

Assess issue and PR separately; no compulsory companion issue. Apply confirmed takeover/rejection within
its scope, not optional suggestions; follow latest evidence-backed direction.

## Withdrawal authority and completion

Explicit withdrawal authority covers in-scope authored open issues/unmerged PRs, not others/merged work,
reopen/merge/branch deletion or cleanup. Preserve dirty/unpushed/private work; carry authorization forward.
Reconcile live state/authorship/new feedback before closing; use factual reason/native retained links,
reliable operation results and ambiguity reconciliation. No courtesy messages; withdrawal authority alone
does not authorize private disclosure. New security findings follow the separate private-reporting rules.
Finish when every captured object has a disposition and feasible authorized actions are done.
Review/CI/decisions/access blockers remain linked follow-up. Apply [records](records.md) and
[resource check](storage.md). Review does not prove initial audit completion or alter membership/positions.
